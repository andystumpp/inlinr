# UX Principles

- **Selection first:** the primary workflow begins from highlighted text, not a blank chat box.
- **Scope should stay visible:** make it obvious what text a request applies to and what will change.
- **Fast feedback:** inline actions should feel quick enough to support iterative writing.
- **Review before apply:** suggested changes should be inspectable before they are committed.
- **Preserve structure:** keep Markdown structure, formatting, and surrounding intent stable unless the user explicitly asks for broader changes.
- **Plain language:** use simple action labels and avoid AI jargon when a direct editing term works better.
- **Keyboard-friendly:** support workflows that feel native inside VS Code.
- **Recoverable experimentation:** make it easy to reject, undo, or refine a suggestion.
- **Context without clutter:** show the right amount of nearby context and diff detail without overwhelming the document.
- **AI attached to text:** the experience should feel like interacting with the document itself, not detouring into a separate tool.
